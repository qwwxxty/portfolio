What goes into a cut04 layers

Everything serves the watch.

01 / 04

  1. Layer 01

    The first second

    Split-screens, title plates, a question on screen. The opening gives people a reason to stay.

  2. Layer 02

    Graphics that explain

    Formulas, charts and animated interfaces build alongside the words, so the idea lands faster.

  3. Layer 03

    Captions with rhythm

    Word-by-word timing, with colour and weight on the words that matter.

  4. Layer 04

    A look for each story

    Grade, type and texture chosen to fit the subject, from a quiet cinematic cut to loud and high-energy.
